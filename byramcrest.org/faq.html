FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why are you having a block party?

A: We have a special geography here, a large cul de sac where people frequently see each other walking, driving, or cycling and want to bring the community together for a day of fun, food, and games! It's a great opportunity to meet your neighbors and enjoy the summer weather.

Q: Who is invited?

A: Everyone is invited!

Q: What if it rains?

A: In the event of rain, the block party will be rescheduled to Sunday, June 7.

Q: How can I help/reply/ask a question?

A: You can contact us by filling out this form

Q: Will there be food?

A: Yes! We will have a variety of food and drinks available, including vegetarian options. If you'd like to bring a dish to share, that would be great but it's not required.

Q: Is this a one-time event?

A: We hope to make this an annual event! If it's successful, we would love to continue it in future years and maybe even expand it to include more of the neighborhood.

Q: Is there a cost to attend?

A: No, the block party is free to attend! We just want to bring the community together and have a good time.

Q: Do I need to bring anything?

A: Just bring yourself and your family! If you'd like to bring a dish or a game to play, that would be great but it's not required.

Q: What activities will there be?

A: We will have a variety of games and activities, including a bouncy house and cornhole.

Q: Are pets allowed?

A: Yes, pets are welcome (as long as they get along with other animals and people)! Please keep them on a leash and clean up after them.

Q: Who is organizing this?

A: The block party is being organized by The Puskar's (17 High Street) and the Anderson's (15 High Street)
